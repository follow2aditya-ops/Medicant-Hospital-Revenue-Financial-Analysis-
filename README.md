# Medicant-Hospital-Revenue-Financial-Analysis-
Conducted a financial and strategic analysis of Medicant Hospital's proposed healthcare membership/coupon scheme as part of my MBA internship project
I worked on a financial and strategic analysis of the hospital's ₹30,000 Healthcare Membership Scheme a prepaid plan offering members benefits worth up to ₹1,50,000 over 5 years.

A few things I actually built and learned along the way:

Financial modeling break-even analysis, Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) modeling, and a 3-scenario (conservative/base/optimistic) revenue projection model for potential corporate/institutional partnerships, estimating this channel alone could contribute a meaningful share of annual scheme revenue.

Excel, hands-on pivot tables, nested formulas, projection tables, and data cleaning-moving from theory to actually building usable financial models.

Strategic frameworks - SWOT, PESTEL, Porter's Five Forces, and the BCG Matrix, and how consultants structure a case: clarify the objective, lay out the structure, state a hypothesis, then test it.

Hospital operations & compliance - how OPD/IPD registration and billing actually flow, AR/AP processes, and regulatory basics like GST slabs and MSME classification that touch every vendor transaction.

Communication & leadership the idea that communication is

fundamentally listening, principles of negotiation, and the different leadership styles (autocratic, democratic, laissez-faire) I saw play out in a real team.

Grateful to the Finance, Legal & Compliance team at Medicant Hospital for the mentorship and for trusting me with real numbers, not just theory.

Looking forward to carrying these lessons into the next chapter of my MBA - and eventually, into a career.
